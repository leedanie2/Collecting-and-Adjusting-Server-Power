# Research deliverables

The [October 2026 poster](Poster/README.md) presents the current corrected grid
findings. Its [data guide](../Code/grid-simulation/POSTER_DATA.md) documents the
scoring windows, cost definitions, uncertainty, and reproducible data sources.

| Deliverable | Status |
|---|---|
| [Poster PPTX and PDF](Poster/README.md) | Current presentation of corrected results |
| [Paper PDF and ODT](Paper/README.md) | Historical manuscript; grid conclusions superseded in part |
| [Presentation PDFs](Presentations/README.md) | Historical presentations; preliminary conclusions superseded |

The corrected reductions in grid-facing CV are 91.4 ± 1.4% for ramp & fill,
8.2 ± 3.5% for the power smoother, and 21.2 ± 12.7% for the governor. These are
means across three workloads with run-to-run sample SD over four repeats, after
a 30 s warm-up. Full-trace runtime and CPU-energy costs still apply. In the
simulated fleet-size sweep, voltage crosses the study threshold first.

The original paper and slide files are preserved as historical artifacts.
Their adjacent READMEs identify the outdated passages and point to the corrected
analysis; the original files themselves have not been revised.
